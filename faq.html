Info & FAQ

Tickets, opening hours, camping, parking and the shuttle bus. BIG SUMMER FEST 2027 · 29–31 July 2027 · Zelená Voda, Nové Mesto nad Váhom, Slovakia.

Buy a ticket – €99.90

Tickets are on sale online via SUPERTICKET.sk and on the official festival website. The limited presale is on now – a 3-day ticket for €99.90.

Yes, tickets will also be sold on site at the festival entrance.

  • 1-day entry: €80
  • 3-day entry: €160

Parking and other services will cost the same on site as online.

With a VIP ticket you enjoy the festival from an exclusive spot with the best view. The raised grandstand gives you a perfect view of the main stage.

  • Private VIP section and separate grandstand with a top view of the stage
  • Separate entrance and toilets
  • Free VIP parking
  • Private bar

VIP tickets are limited to 400.

A PREMIUM ticket gives you access to a reserved standing area right in front of the stage, so you can enjoy the concerts up close – with a better view and less of a crush.

PREMIUM tickets are limited to 1,200. You can upgrade your ticket online or on site.

Yes. Children under 10 (up to 140 cm) get in free. Holders of a Slovak disability card (ZŤP) also get in free – this applies to wheelchair users only.

We can only guarantee tickets bought through superticket.sk or the official festival website. If you have a ticket from another seller without a valid code, you may be refused entry, and the organiser accepts no responsibility for such tickets.

  • Thursday 29 July 2027 – from 15:00
  • Friday 30 July 2027 – from 12:00
  • Saturday 31 July 2027 – from 12:00

The programme starts later. Festival ticket holders do not pay the Zelená Voda resort entry fee. Times are approximate and subject to change.

Entry to the Zelená Voda resort follows the resort's regular opening hours.

Thursday 29 July 2027entry from 15:00 · programme 18:00 – 01:00
Friday 30 July 2027entry from 12:00 · programme 14:00 – 03:00
Saturday 31 July 2027entry from 12:00 · programme 13:00 – 03:00

Times are approximate and subject to change.

The same times apply as for the 3-day ticket, depending on the day you choose. When the programme ends, visitors who are not staying on site should leave the festival zone.

Everyone must show a valid ticket at the entrance. You don't need to print your ticket – having it on your phone is enough.

At the entrance you will get a wristband. Don't take it off, or you won't be let back in. Show the wristband when you re-enter and whenever security staff ask for it.

There will be two entrance gates – one for VIP guests and one for visitors with standard and PREMIUM tickets.

Yes, there are 100 caravan pitches available. More information and booking at beachbarrezort.sk.

The tent villages are reserved for festival visitors only. You can book a tent pitch at beachbarrezort.sk.

Four car parks (P1 – P4) will be available at the festival. Parking for the whole festival is cheapest online via SUPERTICKET.sk. VIP ticket holders have parking by the main entrance included. Visitors with disabilities buy parking separately.

Parking can also be bought on site, but we recommend booking it in advance.

Yes, you can buy parking in advance via SUPERTICKET.sk. You can leave the external car parks P1 – P4 at any time.

Guests staying or camping through beachbarrezort.sk park inside the festival site – but can only drive out after the festival ends.

Yes, a shuttle bus will run from Thursday to Saturday:

  • Nové Mesto nad Váhom, main station ↔ BIG SUMMER FEST + car park
  • Nové Mesto nad Váhom, Tesco ↔ BIG SUMMER FEST + car park

For 2027, together with CK DAKA, we are adding more daytime and night-time connections, including from Piešťany and Trenčín. More stops and times will be announced.

Zelená Voda is right next to the D1 motorway (exit Nové Mesto nad Váhom). By car it is about 1 hour from Bratislava and about 2 hours from Vienna or Brno. The nearest major airports are Bratislava and Vienna. By train, Nové Mesto nad Váhom lies on the main Bratislava – Žilina line, and the festival shuttle runs from the station.

So far we have confirmed VENGABOYS, LOONA, LAS KETCHUP, REDNEX and LUNETIC. More than 35 artists will perform – we're revealing more names step by step on Facebook and Instagram.

No, you may not bring your own alcohol onto the festival site. For what you definitely should pack, see our festival checklist.
