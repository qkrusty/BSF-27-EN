Vengaboys

Netherlands · eurodance · since 1997

Dutch eurodance legends from Rotterdam. The group was founded in 1997 by producers Danski and Delmundo, and its records have sold an estimated 25 million copies worldwide.

The single Boom, Boom, Boom, Boom!! went to No. 1 in the UK, the Netherlands and New Zealand. Their trademark is the Vengabus – the party bus from the hit We Like to Party! (The Vengabus).

They will make their BIG SUMMER FEST debut in 2027.

Greatest hits

  1. Boom, Boom, Boom, Boom!!
  2. We're Going to Ibiza!
  3. We Like to Party! (The Vengabus)
  4. Up & Down
  5. Shalala Lala

Loona

Netherlands · latin dance · “Bailando” 1998

Dutch singer Marie-José van der Kolk was born in 1974 in IJmuiden. She started her career in the 1990s in Mallorca alongside DJ Sammy.

Her song Bailando from 1998 spent eight weeks at No. 1 in Germany and won the Echo award for best international dance single. She also topped the German chart with Hijo de la Luna.

Both singles went gold and platinum in Germany, Austria and Switzerland.

Greatest hits

  1. Bailando
  2. Hijo de la Luna
  3. Latino Lover
  4. Vamos a la playa

Las Ketchup

Spain · pop · “Aserejé” 2002

A Spanish group from Córdoba founded by the sisters Lucía, Lola and Pilar Muñoz – daughters of flamenco musician El Tomate.

Their Aserejé (The Ketchup Song) from 2002 hit No. 1 in dozens of countries including Spain, the UK, Germany, Italy and France, selling more than 7 million copies.

In 2006 they represented Spain at Eurovision. This will be their very first show in Slovakia.

Greatest hits

  1. Aserejé (The Ketchup Song)
  2. Kusha las payas
  3. Un Blodymary

Rednex

Sweden · country & eurodance · since 1994

A Swedish group that has been mixing country with eurodance since 1994, performing with a tongue-in-cheek rural American image.

Cotton Eye Joe became a No. 1 hit in several European countries and also made the US Billboard chart. Other chart-toppers were Old Pop in an Oak and Wish You Were Here from the debut album Sex & Violins (1995).

In 2000 they reached No. 1 in Germany with The Spirit of the Hawk.

Greatest hits

  1. Cotton Eye Joe
  2. Old Pop in an Oak
  3. Wish You Were Here
  4. The Spirit of the Hawk
  5. Hold Me for a While

Lunetic

Czech Republic · boy band · since 1995

A Czech boy band founded in 1995 that drove thousands of fans wild in the late 1990s. Their breakthrough came with the hit Máma from 1998 and the debut album Cik Cak. They made it all the way to 3rd place in the Český slavík music poll.

After splitting up in 2003, the band returned in 2008. In 2025 they celebrated 30 years together and released their first new single in 14 years, Sdílej.

Today they perform as a trio: Václav Jelínek, Aleš Lehký and David Škach.

Greatest hits

  1. Máma
  2. Nechci být sám
  3. Dotyky
  4. Chtěl bych tě líbat
  5. Ať je hudba tvůj lék

BIG SUMMER FEST 2027 announces big news: a Vengaboys premiere, returning favourites and an expanded site

Zelená Voda near Nové Mesto nad Váhom is gearing up for another big summer music ride. BIG SUMMER FEST 2027 takes place from Thursday 29 July to Saturday 31 July 2027 and enters its third edition aiming to build on a successful 2026 – with more space, more comfort and new musical experiences.

Even bigger after a successful 2026

Festival accommodation and parking – everything you need to know

This article refers to the 2026 edition. Accommodation and parking information for 2027 will be published soon.

Dear friends and visitors, the festival weekend at Zelená Voda is fast approaching. So that you can enjoy your stay in maximum comfort and without unnecessary worries, we have prepared some important organisational news.

Accommodation: your comfort right on site

Accommodation is sold exclusively through our partner Beach Bar Rezort Zelená Voda. Stay right in the heart of the festival and have the best performances literally within reach!

  • Festival tents (Fest Camp): ready-made tents right on site in three versions – Black, Fresh or Classic.
  • Caravan pitches: a dedicated festival area reserved especially for caravans.

Extra services: an airbed, a locker for valuables and parking right next to your tent.

Parking: simpler and fairer

  • One price for everyone: we scrapped daily fees – parking for the whole festival cost a flat €25.
  • Simpler choice: parking is bought once and is valid for the whole festival weekend.
  • VIP zone: a limited number of VIP spaces right by the entrance. VIP ticket holders park free.

Book your accommodation and parking in time – capacity goes fast. See you at Zelená Voda!

A festival by the water? Here's your packing checklist (and what not to forget)

A summer festival, water, music, nightlife and a rented tent waiting for you right at Zelená Voda? Sounds like the perfect weekend at BIG SUMMER FEST! Even though you don't need to bring a tent, there are a few things you should pack to enjoy the festival to the full. Here's your little festival survival guide.

Sleeping basics

  • Sleeping bag or blanket
  • Sleeping mat or air mattress
  • Small pillow

Clothing: festival fashion vs. reality

  • Swimwear
  • Comfortable daytime clothes
  • Something warmer for the evening
  • Waterproof jacket or poncho
  • Comfortable shoes and flip-flops or sandals

Hygiene that will save you

  • Towel
  • Plastic bags or waterproof pouches
  • Toilet paper or wet wipes
  • Toothbrush, toothpaste, deodorant, shower gel
  • Sunscreen and insect repellent

Handy extras

  • Sunglasses and a cap or hat
  • Small torch or headlamp
  • Basic first-aid kit
  • Power bank

Extra comfort

  • Blanket or seat pad
  • Small backpack or bum bag

Documents

  • ID card or passport
  • Health insurance card (EHIC)

What not to bring?

Your own alcohol – it is not allowed on the festival site.

And above all: a good mood, an open mind and the desire to experience something unforgettable. That's what matters most at a festival!

The Zelená Voda resort in Nové Mesto nad Váhom

Imagine summer, music, sunshine, water and endless good vibes. Imagine a festival you will never forget – BIG SUMMER FEST.

The Zelená Voda resort is tucked away just a few minutes from town, yet arriving here feels like stepping into a completely different world. A crystal-clear lake surrounded by greenery, wide lawns and beaches make it the ideal place for a summer festival.

What awaits you on site

  • Beach bar
  • Paddleboards
  • Beach volleyball and football
  • Cycling and hiking in the area

Festival zones

  • Main stage
  • Chill-out zone with bean bags under fairy lights
  • Talk shows and Q&As with artists
  • Food court with international cuisine
  • Space for sunbathing and swimming in the lake

The surroundings

The Trenčín region has the densest network of cycle routes in Slovakia – up to 750 km – plus plenty of hiking trails. Nearby you can also visit castles and manor houses.

Water quality

The water quality rating at Zelená Voda has improved from “good” to the highest category, “excellent”.
